      What shows up here Lower than usual. A student’s last 3 check-ins are all further from 1 than their usual number. Their usual is the number they get most often. They need at least 6 check-ins, and the last 3 must fall within the last 5 school days. No check-in lately. A student has no check-in in the last 5 school days while the rest of the class does. A weekday pattern. One weekday’s average is noticeably further from 1 than the class’s overall average. This needs at least 20 check-ins.
