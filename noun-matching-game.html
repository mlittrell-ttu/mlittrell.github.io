The three kinds of nouns

A noun names a person, a place or a thing.

Person

A person is someone, like a teacher, a mom or a firefighter.

teacher · sister · doctor

Place

A place is somewhere you can go, like a park or a school.

park · kitchen · Texas

Thing

A thing is something you can see or touch, like a pencil or a ball.

pencil · apple · bike
