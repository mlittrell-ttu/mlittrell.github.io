How to download

Keep any app on your computer so it works even when the Wi-Fi doesn't. Nothing to install.

  1. 1

    Press Download

    Pick an app above and press its Download button. One small file saves to your Downloads folder.

  2. 2

    Put it somewhere handy

    Move the file to your desktop, a class folder or a USB drive. You can rename it if you like.

  3. 3

    Double-click to open

    It opens in your web browser and works right away, with or without internet.

Download blocked on a school computer? Open the app instead, then press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on a Mac) and save it as "Webpage, HTML only". You can also share the file with colleagues by email or a shared drive.

Made by educators, for educators.

Teacher Apps is a collection of applications built by educators, for the classrooms they know best. Every app is free, works offline, has no ads and needs no account, so it fits any school, whatever the network or the budget.

The project is led by Michael Littrell, a former K-12 teacher and senior product manager in the educational software industry, now working as an Assistant Professor at Tennessee Tech University.
